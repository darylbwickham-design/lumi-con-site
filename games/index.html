Bring your crew

One drives.
Everyone gets you out.

Open the game, enter your callsign, then choose Host Expedition. Share the six-character room code or copy an invite link. Friends choose Join Crew.

The mountain road includes seven stacked overpasses, fourteen expedition obstacles and two shortcuts. Repair bridges plank by plank, clear fallen trees and rockslides, and restore generators to open the service gates. Eight dinosaur species share the island; keep a lookout and build your field journal.

Scout the next turn or rig the winch while your driver eases up the muddy climb in low gear. Share the work to finish faster. At checkpoint camps, save recovery progress, refuel and trade salvage for supplies and upgrades. Watch brake heat, coolant, punctured tires and loose cargo on the long descent.
