Release notes and checksums

To use previews through Pulse Weaver, open Studio → Updates and opt in to Alpha. Version 1.13.3 also provides the separate Unstable option. You can also download either preview from its release page. The regular channel remains the default for new installs.

The installer includes Pulse Weaver's YouTube desktop app registration. Click Connect YouTube to sign in with your own account; no developer credentials are needed. Updates preserve existing registrations and settings. Google's verification and account restrictions still apply.

Updating? Close Pulse Weaver and any previous installer, then run setup. Do not uninstall first. Setup creates a verified app-and-settings backup and supports rollback. Keep your backups private: they can contain account information.

Your accounts, your choice

Connecting YouTube and other platforms.

Pulse Weaver uses YouTube API Services. Connecting Google lets the desktop app access the channel and live-stream information needed for the features you authorise, including broadcast management, live chat and requested moderation. You sign in with Google in your browser; this website does not ask for your Google password.

Settings and connected-account credentials are stored locally. Optional connected services receive information needed for their features; the hosted Kick relay handles short-lived event delivery. Read the Privacy Policy for details, retention and deletion options.

You can disconnect in Pulse Weaver or revoke Google access. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Publication of this page does not imply Google verification or endorsement.
