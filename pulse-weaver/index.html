Inside the studio

From scene setup to going live.

Landscape and portrait

Compose 16:9 and 9:16 scenes with separate canvases, sources and transforms for each format.

Live show control

Run Stages and animated Looks from the refined Show/Control workspace, with platform-colour format sliders, audio monitoring and stinger transitions. Build my show prompts for the inputs your selected layouts need.

Transparent HUD and alert layers

Project one independent, transparent HUD onto a chosen display. Add browser and media effects, and reuse up to three alert layers per format in your show. The unfinished overlay editor and Lights controls remain reserved for a future update.

Chat and Lumia keypad

Combine streaming chats and Discord through the Pulse Discord bot, or switch the chat panel to a 15-button Lumia keypad. Assign names and icons, then bind the easy-to-find Keypad 01–15 alerts in Lumia. Platform availability depends on permissions and setup.

Discord chat · Powered by Pulse

Your Discord channels, inside Pulse Weaver.

Pulse is the Discord bot behind Pulse Weaver's Discord chat feature. Read up to five selected channels from one server alongside Twitch, YouTube and Kick in the main chat panel. Public and private moderator channels are available where both your account and Pulse have access.

Have Pulse added to your server with permission to read the selected channels. In Pulse Weaver, open the chat panel's options menu → Discord channels…, approve the connection in your browser, then choose your server and channels. Discord is receive-only; the composer continues to send to the streaming platforms. Choose Discord, an individual channel or All chats (no Discord) to control what appears.

YouTube incoming messages automatically use the chat scanner in the combined Twitch/YouTube/Kick chat box. Sending, deletion, timeouts and bans use the official API. No reader dropdown or pasted chat links are needed. Private or restricted chat may be unavailable to the scanner.

The earlier Windows alpha and unstable builds are now superseded by 2.0.0 for the regular installation. Older releases and the separate isolated 2 Preview installer remain in the release history.

An experimental Mac preview is available for Apple Silicon on macOS 13 or later. It is not Apple-notarized; read its release instructions before installing.

Existing regular, alpha and regular unstable users can find 2.0.0 through Studio → Updates. The separately installed 2 Preview remains isolated and is not silently migrated. Lite and the Mac preview are separate editions.

The installer includes Pulse Weaver's YouTube Desktop app registration. Leave both optional app fields empty to use it, or enter your own client ID and secret in Action → Connections → YouTube. The bundled app's daily API quota is shared across its users; if it runs out, API control actions stop until the daily reset. A personal project still has limits. The in-app guide explains setup, quota and Google's Testing restrictions. Bundled credentials are never displayed in the editor. Read the YouTube setup notes.

Updating? Close Pulse Weaver and any previous installer, then run setup. Do not uninstall first. Setup creates a verified app-and-settings backup and supports rollback. Keep your backups private: they can contain account information.

Your accounts, your choice

Connecting YouTube and other platforms.

Pulse Weaver uses YouTube API Services. Connecting Google lets the desktop app access the channel and live-stream information needed for the features you authorise, including broadcast management, live chat and requested moderation. You sign in with Google in your browser; this website does not ask for your Google password.

Settings and connected-account credentials are stored locally. Optional connected services receive information needed for their features; the hosted Kick relay handles short-lived event delivery. Read the Privacy Policy for details, retention and deletion options.

You can disconnect in Pulse Weaver or revoke Google access. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Publication of this page does not imply Google verification or endorsement.
